# The Powers of the First War

Every legend carries two abilities. Their **Signature Power** is their domain made literal — always active in every battle, growing as they level. Their **Ultimate** is a rare, once-per-season, last-ditch move: it scales with how badly their side is *losing*, so it is weak when ahead and devastating when behind — a comeback only, never lead-padding. Rival legends carry powers built to counter each other.

## ⚔ The Vanguard (Heroes)

### Cassia Dawn — "The First Light"
- **Signature — Dawnline:** Every Vanguard ally in her sightline fights with first-light clarity — steadier nerves, unbroken formation, cleaner reads — deepening each turn she keeps her feet. It is presence, not force: she makes everyone around her miss less.
- **Ultimate — Breaking Dawn:** She calls the sunrise; the further the Vanguard trails, the more blinding the light, wiping the enemy's momentum and re-setting the field to even in proportion to the deficit. A flicker with a lead, a total tempo reversal near collapse — then spent.

### Titus Vale — "The Ironhide Sentinel"
- **Signature — Ironset:** He sets as a living wall and absorbs damage aimed at anyone behind him, the plating thickening the longer he holds; hold long enough and almost nothing gets through — but the instant he moves, it resets.
- **Ultimate — The Last Rampart:** When the Vanguard is overrun he raises a rampart whose height is the deficit — the worse the rout, the taller the wall — stopping exactly one enemy wave cold, then it crumbles.

### Juno Reyes — "The Skybreaker"
- **Signature — Skybreak:** She acts a full beat before anyone can answer, each clean tempo sharpening her edge to a hard floor. Pure initiative — she's simply already there. Steal a tempo back and the sharpening resets.
- **Ultimate — Terminal Velocity:** She collapses the distance between where the Vanguard is and where they need to be, the acceleration scaling to how far behind — a field-length blur that erases the gap when buried, then her legs are gone.

### Marcus Kell — "The Long Reckoning"
- **Signature — The Long Reckoning:** He soaks punishment without breaking and grows steadier the longer a battle drags; force spends itself against him while time stays on his side. The pure counter to Kord Malphas — built to still be standing after the biggest hit.
- **Ultimate — Attrition's End:** Nearly finished, he returns the toll he has absorbed as a single reckoning — throttled to nothing while comfortable, an avalanche scaled to the beating he took when on the ropes.

### Sana Okoye — "The Tidewarden"
- **Signature — Tidewarden:** The tide turns for her exactly when it matters — routine moments flat, but as the finish closes she rises, steady while the water is at everyone's throat. The standing answer to Delphine Roe: the one player rigged odds can't account for.
- **Ultimate — The Turning Tide:** In a closing moment with the Vanguard behind, she guarantees one decisive play — a single hinge the battle turns on — the longer the odds, the larger the swing.

### Eli Thorne — "The Oathkeeper"
- **Signature — Oathkeeper:** Every vow he speaks aloud becomes binding — to hold a line, reach a mark, strike a foe — hardening into force that won't let him fall short of his word. He cannot be knocked off an oath he has sworn.
- **Ultimate — The Kept Oath:** With the Vanguard losing, he calls due every oath owed him — the sworn pulled back to their marks and locked to their promised roles, the reach scaling to how far his side has fallen.

### Rook Callahan — "The Undrafted"
- **Signature — The Undrafted:** He carries no gift but refusal — intimidation, fear, and reputation slide off him, and he climbs back from hits that put others down for good. He cannot be made to believe he is beaten.
- **Ultimate — Nothing To Lose:** For one decisive stretch he cannot be put down — and the more thoroughly the Vanguard has been written off, the longer the refusal holds and the further it spreads to the men beside him.

### Lyra Vann — "The Signal"
- **Signature — The Signal:** She reads intent a beat before it moves — the opponent's true next action surfacing just early enough to answer. The direct counter to Nyx Sable, whose feints are the static her signal cuts through.
- **Ultimate — Clear Signal:** With the Vanguard behind, she burns off every layer of deception on the field and shows her side the truth of it, the revelation growing with how badly they've been misled.

### Brother Amos — "The Shepherd"
- **Signature — The Shepherd:** He guides allies clear of danger — repositioning the exposed, steadying the faltering — so no Vanguard soul strays alone into a trap. He stands against Saint Vega, the false shepherd whose gilded promises lure the same flock.
- **Ultimate — The Gathering:** With the Vanguard scattered and losing, he calls the strayed and fallen back into formation, the number he can gather scaling to how broken the line has become.

### Gideon Frost — "The Last Stand"
- **Signature — The Last Stand:** Alone among the twenty, his comeback is his everyday power — his strength scales inversely to his side's standing every battle, so the closer the Vanguard is to defeat, the more devastating he becomes. The nightmare of Countess Mave, whose siphon only feeds him.
- **Ultimate — Zero Hour:** At the very edge of annihilation his signature reaches its absolute expression — near-certain defeat converts into one overwhelming stand, the force a mirror of how completely his side was about to lose. Unusable with any cushion at all.

## 🐍 The Dominion (Villains)

### Roman Slate — "The Dynasty"
- **Signature — The Dynasty:** Every advantage compounds into the next — leads snowball, order entrenches — but the climb flattens toward a ceiling, and the moment the Dominion falls behind the whole edifice starts to crumble until he claws the lead back. Far easier to beat early than late. The antithesis of Cassia Dawn's fresh start.
- **Ultimate — Reckoning Of Kings:** When the Dominion is unexpectedly losing, he reasserts the old order and reclaims ground in proportion to how far the upstarts pushed him back — toothless when he rules, a crushing restoration when his dynasty is threatened.

### Vesper Kane — "The Turnover Queen"
- **Signature — The Reaching Hand:** She strips power straight out of an opponent's hands mid-motion and wears it for a breath. Theft as tempo — every possession is a loan she can call in. Her one bad matchup is Ezra Cain, who can seal a possession un-stealable.
- **Ultimate — The Great Theft:** With the Dominion behind, she steals the opposing side's accumulated momentum wholesale, the haul scaling to how large a lead they'd built — a total heist of a winning team's advantage when she's losing.

### Silas Vane — "The Rust King"
- **Signature — The Rust King:** Everything he touches corrodes — enemy armor, defenses, and structure degrade a little more each exchange. Decay as slow certainty; what he can't break he ruins. The brother-poison to Titus Vale: iron is what rust was made to eat.
- **Ultimate — Total Corrosion:** With the Dominion losing, he accelerates the rot across the whole field at once, stripping enemy defenses in proportion to how far behind he trails.

### Kord Malphas — "The Breaker"
- **Signature — The Breaker:** Raw, unsubtle force — he drives straight through defenses built to hold, each barrier he shatters lending weight to the next. The wall's answer. He crashes forever against Marcus Kell, built to still be standing after the biggest hit.
- **Ultimate — Breaking Point:** With the Dominion behind, he winds up one blow that annihilates — taking the enemy's single strongest defensive structure off the field for the battle, the deeper the deficit the larger the bulwark it breaks.

### Nyx Sable — "The Whisper"
- **Signature — The Whisper:** She sows false signals — feints, misdirection, planted reads — so opponents commit to the wrong answer and never see the real one until too late. Deception as a fog she controls. Lyra Vann's opposite number.
- **Ultimate — The Long Con:** With the Dominion losing, she reveals the whole battle to have been a setup and springs it, the reversal scaling to how confidently the enemy committed to their lead.

### Ezra Cain — "The Fallen Warden"
- **Signature — The Fallen Warden:** He seals a possession shut so it cannot be stripped — ball, position, advantage held under a warden's grip that theft slides off. He guarded the Vanguard's vault for years and turned that discipline against every hand reaching in. Vesper Kane's direct counter.
- **Ultimate — The Knife's Turn:** With the Dominion losing, he engineers a betrayal that turns an enemy asset to his side for the rest of the battle — its value scaling to how commanding the lead he's clawing at — then no one else will follow him that season.

### Countess Mave — "The Vampire"
- **Signature — The Long Thirst:** She drains strength directly from opponents, growing stronger exactly as they weaken, so a long engagement bleeds them into her. Siphon as slow inevitability. Her one bad matchup is Gideon Frost, whose strength rises the more she drains him.
- **Ultimate — Last Drop:** With the Dominion behind, she drains the single strongest enemy on the field, the amount scaling to how far she trails — a near-total exsanguination of their best when she's losing.

### Saint Vega — "The Gilded"
- **Signature — The Gilded:** He hoards — accumulating advantage and dangling gilded promises that tempt wavering allies to defect toward him. Greed as gravity. The counterfeit of Brother Amos, a false shepherd whose flock is bought, not guided.
- **Ultimate — Reckoning Of Gold:** With the Dominion losing, he cashes out to buy the battle back — the payout set first by how far behind his side has fallen, his hoard only sweetening it within a fixed ceiling and never enough on its own while he's comfortable.

### Dr. Orin Pyre — "The Negation"
- **Signature — The Ashing:** A slow pyre burns around him — powers brought near it gutter and misfire; once per battle he can snuff a single ability outright for a few beats. Suppression as hard counter — he starves strength of air. He is the answer key to every gift on the board, though every gift comes back.
- **Ultimate — Ashfall:** With the Dominion losing, he lets the pyre swell and takes the enemy's strongest active power to ash for the rest of the battle, the reach scaling to how far behind he trails.

### Delphine Roe — "The Oddsmaker"
- **Signature — The Oddsmaker:** She sets the odds and bends them — near-misses become makes for the Dominion, coin-flips land on her call, the improbable quietly favors her side. Fate as a tilted table. Her constant frustration is Sana Okoye, who keeps hitting the shot the odds forbid.
- **Ultimate — House Always Wins:** With the Dominion behind, she calls in the long-shot and forces it to land, the payout scaling to how steep the deficit — a miraculous against-the-odds swing when she's losing.

## Rival oppositions (powers built to counter)
- **Cassia Dawn ↔ Roman Slate** — fresh first light vs entrenched dynasty.
- **Titus Vale ↔ Silas Vane** — iron wall vs the rust that eats iron *(brothers)*.
- **Marcus Kell ↔ Kord Malphas** — endurance that outlasts the one huge hit vs the Breaker who throws it.
- **Sana Okoye ↔ Delphine Roe** — the clutch that beats the odds vs the fate that sets them.
- **Brother Amos ↔ Saint Vega** — the true shepherd vs the false one who buys the flock.
- **Gideon Frost ↔ Countess Mave** — strength from weakness vs the siphon that only feeds it.
- **Lyra Vann ↔ Nyx Sable** — the signal that cuts the fog vs the whisper that makes it.
- **Vesper Kane ↔ Ezra Cain** — the theft vs the possession that refuses to turn over *(she turned him)*.
