# Season One — The First War (the opening saga)

This is the Season 1 opening of The First War, told in six beats.

## Panel 1 — The Throne
No one alive remembers the last time the throne changed hands. **Roman Slate**, The Dynasty, has held the Dominion so long the war forgot it could end. "Rebellion? I don't fear it. I collect it."

## Panel 2 — The Thinning Light
The Vanguard has never been fewer. **Cassia Dawn**, The First Light, counts what remains and does not flinch — the fewer the torches, the brighter each has to burn. "Fewer torches. Same fire. Hold the line."

## Panel 3 — Brother Against Brother
**Titus Vale**, the Ironhide Sentinel, holds the line. Across it stands the brother who left it to rust — **Silas Vane**, the Rust King. "Iron rusts, brother. Everything I touch does."

## Panel 4 — The Knife You Know
**Ezra Cain** wore Vanguard blue once. Then **Vesper Kane**, the Turnover Queen, showed him what betrayal pays — and he became the Fallen Warden. "I don't beat you. I let you beat yourself."

## Panel 5 — Every Matchup a Front
There is no schedule here. Only fronts. Every week, somewhere, one side's war is won and another's is lost — and the map is redrawn by Sunday night.

## Panel 6 — The Season Gets a Vote
The ending isn't written. This year a new class of legends is cast — and every manager who chooses a side becomes one of them. The season itself gets a vote. Which side are you?

## The inaugural league
The first real league to fight The First War is a league also named **The First War**, commissioned by Nate. Its managers are the first real class cast into the canon. Their draft is Season 1's opening battle.
