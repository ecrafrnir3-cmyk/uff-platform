SUBJECT: The front moved back to zero — and here's why

Managers,

The war map lied to you for a week. I'm fixing it in the open rather than
quietly, because you all saw it.

WHAT IT SAID: Dominion ahead, front at -2.
WHAT IT SAYS NOW: 0. Dead even. Three fights each.

WHAT HAPPENED

The front was supposed to track one thing: Vanguard teams beating Dominion
teams on the field. Six of those have happened. Heroes took two in Week 1,
the Dominion took two in Week 2. 3-3. Nobody has gained an inch.

But the map wasn't only counting those. It was also counting the Free
Legends — the six characters nobody drafted, the ones who come out of the
field to ambush a claimed champion. Those ambushes were being scored as
captured ground. Four of them landed, mostly for the Dominion, and that is
the entire -2. Not one real game was ever counted wrong.

So: an unmanaged character nobody rosters was taking territory off you. That
was never the rule. It's not the rule now.

WHAT THE RULE IS

Only a Vanguard-vs-Dominion head-to-head moves the front, and it's decided
by the score on the board. Nothing else. Not a simulation, not an ambush, not
a duel inside your own faction. If you want ground, take it off the other
side in a real game.

The ambushes still happen and still get written up. They just don't move the
line anymore — the story will tell you so when it says "the front did not
move."

WHAT THIS DOES NOT TOUCH

Nothing that counts. Records, standings, points, matchup results, Restore
Chips — all untouched, all exactly where they were. This is the war map and
only the war map.

WHAT IT MEANS

Two weeks of everything you've got, and the line is where it started. Both
sides threw their best and neither one gained a yard. That's not a
correction, that's a deadlock — and it's the truer story anyway.

Week 3 kicks off tonight. Somebody go take some ground.

— The Supreme Commissioner
