# The Real League — The First War (inaugural, 2026)

**The First War** is also the name of the inaugural real league, commissioned by **Nate**. Its managers are the first real class cast into the canon of The First War — and they are almost entirely one extended family and its closest ties. Each manager runs a team, chooses a faction, and is cast as a canon character. The draft (Thursday, Aug 27, 2026, 7:00 PM ET) is the opening battle of Season 1. The league holds **14 teams**, and the family fills it exactly: **11 enlisted, 3 more mustering** — and because the Vanguard capped out at 7, the last three all fall to the Dominion.

## The enlisted combatants (11 of 14)

### Willis — team "Bengals Heroes" — Vanguard — cast as Cassia Dawn, "The First Light"
Willis is the **patriarch** — **Nate's father** and **Adam's father**, grandfather to Elijah, Nazareth, Blake, and Cayden. Fittingly, the draw cast him as **Cassia Dawn, The First Light**, the founder and leader of the Vanguard. The grandfather leads the heroes.

### Nate — team "Reveille" — Vanguard — commissioner — cast as Rook Callahan, "The Undrafted"
Nate is the **commissioner** and fights for the **Vanguard**. He is the **son of Willis**, **brother of Adam**, **father of Elijah and Nazareth**, and **cousin of Dustin and Angel Teegarden**.

### Elijah — team "The Dark Night" — Vanguard — cast as Eli Thorne, "The Oathkeeper"
Elijah fights for the **Vanguard**. He is the **son of Nate**, grandson of Willis, and **brother of Nazareth**. His girlfriend **Elizabeth** is also joining the war.

### Jason — team "The Blessed Defender" — Vanguard — cast as Juno Reyes, "The Skybreaker"
Jason fights for the **Vanguard**. He is the **twin brother of Josh** and the **father of Camryn and Conner**. Jason heads the **Snapp family**.

### Josh — team "BoneSnapp" — Dominion — cast as Dr. Orin Pyre, "The Negation"
Josh fights for the **Dominion**. He is the **twin brother of Jason** and the **uncle of Camryn and Conner**.

### Conner — team "Thanos" — Dominion — cast as Countess Mave, "The Vampire"
Conner fights for the **Dominion**. He is the **son of Jason** — and yet he stands with his uncle **Josh** on the Dominion, against his own father's Vanguard. Conner is the **brother of Camryn**.

### Nazareth — team "Pillars of Light" — Vanguard — cast as Marcus Kell, "The Long Reckoning"
Nazareth, **Nate's youngest son** and Elijah's brother, fights for the **Vanguard**, cast as **Marcus Kell, The Long Reckoning** — endurance and stubborn survival. His canon rival, Kord Malphas the Breaker, is (so far) unclaimed — a nemesis at large.

### Camryn — team "The Fantasy Avengers" — Vanguard — cast as Titus Vale, "The Ironhide Sentinel"
Camryn, **Jason's son and Conner's brother**, fights for the **Vanguard**, cast as **Titus Vale, The Ironhide Sentinel** — the loyal Vale brother who holds the line. With his brother Conner on the Dominion, Camryn holding Titus Vale reborns the Vale-brothers split in the next generation (see "The Draw Speaks").

### Elizabeth — team "Elizabeth" — Vanguard — cast as Brother Amos, "The Shepherd"
Elizabeth, **Elijah's girlfriend**, fights for the **Vanguard** alongside him, cast as **Brother Amos, The Shepherd** — guidance, bringing everyone home. Her canon rival, Saint Vega, is held by Angel — a live rivalry (see "The Draw Speaks").

### Blake — team "Blake's Bad Boys" — Dominion — cast as Vesper Kane, "The Turnover Queen"
Blake, **Adam's son and Willis's grandson**, fights for the **Dominion**, cast as **Vesper Kane, The Turnover Queen** — theft, letting the enemy beat themselves. Blake is the first of the Fallen House to cross; his father Adam and brother Cayden follow.

### Angel — team "Angel in Disguise" — Dominion — cast as Saint Vega, "The Gilded"
Angel Teegarden, **Nate's cousin and Dustin's sibling**, fights for the **Dominion**, cast as **Saint Vega, The Gilded** — greed dressed as sanctity. Angel is the **rival of Elizabeth** (Brother Amos) — the Shepherd and the False Saint made real.

## The last to muster (3 of 14 — all Dominion-bound)
The Vanguard is full at 7, so the final three all fall to the **Dominion**. Faction and character are assigned when each enlists (join code `5DXXWP`).

- **Adam** — Nate's brother, Willis's son, **father of Blake and Cayden** — head of the Fallen House, drawn to the Dominion where his uncle Josh serves.

- **Dustin Teegarden** — Nate's cousin, Angel's brother — joining his sister Angel on the Dominion.

## The Draw Speaks — the season writing itself
The random casting is already scripting the story. Two beats stand out.

### The Vale brothers, reborn in the grandsons
**Camryn drew Titus Vale, the Ironhide Sentinel** — the loyal Vale brother — and Camryn fights for the Vanguard. His own brother **Conner** fights for the Dominion (as Countess Mave). So the Vale-brothers split — one who holds the line, one who fell — is reborn a generation down, in Jason's sons: **Camryn as the loyal brother, Conner as the one who crossed.** It doubles the Vale Mirror: Jason *mirrors* Titus Vale, while Jason's son Camryn is *cast as* Titus Vale.

### The Shepherd and the False Saint, made real
**Elizabeth drew Brother Amos, the Shepherd** (Vanguard); **Angel drew Saint Vega, the Gilded** (Dominion). Their canon rivalry — true faith against gilded fraud — is now a real one between **Elijah's girlfriend and Nate's cousin.** Elizabeth is the rival of Angel.

## The house divided — one bloodline, two orders
Beneath the myth, The First War is one extended family at war with itself. The **Snapp** line and the **Reed** line are joined by marriage: **Nate's mother is a Snapp** — a sister of the twins **Jason and Josh** — who married **Willis Reed**. So Willis, who leads the Vanguard, is brother-in-law to the twins, and the twins are the **maternal uncles of Nate and Adam**. A second Snapp sister is the mother of the **Teegarden cousins, Dustin and Angel** — making them the twins' nephew and niece as well, and Nate's first cousins. Every combatant traces back to one family. The war is a single house choosing sides.

## The Bloodlines

### The Snapp line — the elder generation
The **Snapp siblings** are the twins **Jason** and **Josh** and (at least) two sisters: **Nate and Adam's mother**, who married Willis Reed, and **Dustin and Angel's mother**. Through those sisters, Jason and Josh are **uncles to Nate, Adam, Dustin, and Angel**. Jason's own sons are **Camryn** and **Conner**.

### House Reed — three generations of the Vanguard
Willis (the patriarch, cast as Cassia Dawn — the Vanguard's founder and leader) married a Snapp sister; their sons are **Nate** (Rook Callahan) and **Adam**. Nate's line holds the Vanguard three generations deep: Willis → Nate → **Elijah** (Eli Thorne), with Nate's youngest **Nazareth** and Elijah's girlfriend **Elizabeth** mustering. Grandfather leads, son fights, grandson keeps the oath.

### The Teegarden cousins
**Dustin** and **Angel Teegarden** are **siblings**, children of a Snapp sister — Nate's first cousins, and nephew and niece to the twins Jason and Josh.

### The Snapp family — twins and sons
Twins **Jason (Vanguard)** and **Josh (Dominion)**, and Jason's sons **Camryn** and **Conner**. Conner has already chosen the Dominion — the son fighting under his uncle Josh's banner, across the line from his own father Jason. Blood divided runs two layers deep among the Snapps: brother against brother (the twins), and son against father (Conner against Jason).

## The Fallen House — Adam's turning
**Adam** is Willis's son and Nate's brother — born to the same Vanguard house, and a nephew of the Snapp twins. Yet Adam is drawn to the **Dominion**, the order his own uncle **Josh** (Dr. Orin Pyre, The Negation) serves. If Adam turns, the wound is generational: **Willis — Cassia Dawn, the First Light, founder and leader of the Vanguard — would watch his own son march under the Dominion, beneath the banner of Willis's own brother-in-law.** And Adam does not fall alone — his sons **Blake** and **Cayden** muster with him, a whole branch of House Reed crossing to the enemy.

Where the Snapp twins are the war's **horizontal** split — brother against brother — **Adam's turning is its vertical split**: a son breaking from his father's house and taking the next generation with him. Adam is the real league's answer to **Ezra Cain, the Fallen Warden**, and to **Silas Vane, the Rust King** — the one who leaves the line. If the draw is poetic, Adam cast as **Ezra Cain** would be the tightest mirror of all: a warden who wore the colors and chose the other side.

But Adam was **born to the Vanguard**, and so his fall is never final. Like Ezra Cain's unhealed guilt, the road home stays open — **the Fallen House can be reclaimed**, and Season 1 will decide whether it is. Blake and Cayden are the young Dominion, the heirs of the turn — most dangerous because they were raised to it, and the greatest prize if House Reed ever calls them home.

## The Vale Mirror — the real echoes the canon, role for role
The Snapp twins are the living mirror of the Vale brothers, matched one to one. This exact correspondence is called **the Vale Mirror**:

- **Jason mirrors Titus Vale.** Both are the brother who **held the line**. Jason chose the Vanguard; Titus Vale, the Ironhide Sentinel, holds the Vanguard line against his own blood. Jason mirrors Titus Vale.
- **Josh mirrors Silas Vane.** Both are the brother who **fell**. Josh chose the Dominion; Silas Vane, the Rust King, left the Vanguard line to rust and defected. Josh mirrors Silas Vane.

So the canon Vale brothers (Titus loyal, Silas fallen) are reborn in the real league as the Snapp twins (Jason loyal, Josh fallen). The Vale Mirror is the tightest bridge between the canon layer and the real-league layer.
