# The Vanguard — The Order of Heroes (AFC)

The **Vanguard** is the order of heroes in The First War. It is the hero faction, mapped to the AFC. At the start of Season 1 the Vanguard is thinned and outnumbered, holding the line against the Dominion's long reign. Its ten legends:

## Cassia Dawn — "The First Light"
Domain: **Leadership.** Cassia Dawn named the Vanguard and rallies what remains of it. Where she looks, others find their courage. She is the founder and standard-bearer of the Vanguard, and the sworn **rival of Roman Slate**, The Dynasty, who holds the throne she means to break.

## Titus Vale — "The Ironhide Sentinel"
Domain: **Defense.** Skin like iron; where he plants his feet, the line holds. Titus Vale is the **brother of Silas Vane**, the Rust King, who defected to the Dominion. Titus holds the line against his own blood.

## Juno Reyes — "The Skybreaker"
Domain: **Speed.** Faster than doubt — you cannot trap what you cannot catch. Juno Reyes is a Vanguard hero.

## Marcus Kell — "The Long Reckoning"
Domain: **Endurance.** Written off three times, still standing. Marcus Kell is a Vanguard hero of stubborn survival. His rival is **Kord Malphas**, the Breaker — force against endurance; Kell is the load that has never broken.

## Sana Okoye — "The Tidewarden"
Domain: **Clutch.** The bigger the moment, the calmer she gets. Sana Okoye is a Vanguard hero. Her rival is **Delphine Roe**, the Oddsmaker — Sana is the clutch variable the Oddsmaker can never price.

## Eli Thorne — "The Oathkeeper"
Domain: **Honor.** Never bent a rule to win, never will. Eli Thorne is a Vanguard hero and the moral spine of the order.

## Rook Callahan — "The Undrafted"
Domain: **Grit.** Nobody wanted him; now everybody has to face him. Rook Callahan is a Vanguard hero, the overlooked underdog who made himself impossible to ignore.

## Lyra Vann — "The Signal"
Domain: **Foresight.** She sees the play before it is called. Lyra Vann is a Vanguard hero and the **rival of Nyx Sable**, the Whisper of the Dominion — foresight against deception.

## Brother Amos — "The Shepherd"
Domain: **Guidance.** He fights to bring everyone home. Brother Amos is a Vanguard hero who protects and rallies the rest. His rival is **Saint Vega**, the Gilded — the false saint who sells the salvation Amos gives freely.

## Gideon Frost — "The Last Stand"
Domain: **Defiance.** Most dangerous when the scoreboard says he is done. Gideon Frost is a Vanguard hero of last-ditch defiance. His rival is **Countess Mave**, the Vampire — she feeds on strength, but defiance is not a resource, so Gideon is the one man she cannot drain.
