# The Dominion — The Order of Villains (NFC)

The **Dominion** is the order of villains in The First War. It is the villain faction, mapped to the NFC. At the start of Season 1 the Dominion holds the throne and the high ground. Its ten legends:

## Roman Slate — "The Dynasty"
Domain: **Reign.** He does not fear rebellion; he collects rebels. Roman Slate sits the Dominion's throne, unbeaten in living memory. He is the **rival of Cassia Dawn**, The First Light, who leads the Vanguard against him.

## Vesper Kane — "The Turnover Queen"
Domain: **Theft.** She does not beat you; she lets you beat yourself. Vesper Kane trades in others' mistakes. She is the one who **turned Ezra Cain** — she showed the Warden what betrayal pays.

## Silas Vane — "The Rust King"
Domain: **Decay.** Rust spreads faster than steel ever holds. Silas Vane is the **brother of Titus Vale**, the Ironhide Sentinel. Silas left the Vanguard line to rust and defected to the Dominion — brother against brother.

## Kord Malphas — "The Breaker"
Domain: **Force.** He finds what you rely on and breaks it. Kord Malphas is a Dominion villain of raw force. His rival is **Marcus Kell**, the Long Reckoning — the endurance the Breaker cannot break.

## Nyx Sable — "The Whisper"
Domain: **Deception.** You never hear her coming. Nyx Sable is a Dominion villain and the **rival of Lyra Vann**, The Signal — deception against foresight.

## Ezra Cain — "The Fallen Warden"
Domain: **Betrayal.** Vanguard once; he chose the winning side. Ezra Cain was a Vanguard warden before **Vesper Kane turned him** to the Dominion. He is the living proof that the line can be crossed.

## Countess Mave — "The Vampire"
Domain: **Siphon.** She grows strong on borrowed brilliance. Countess Mave is a Dominion villain who feeds on others' success. Her rival is **Gideon Frost**, the Last Stand — the empty cup she cannot drain.

## Saint Vega — "The Gilded"
Domain: **Greed.** Glory shared, to Vega, is glory wasted. Saint Vega is a Dominion villain of gilded excess. His rival is **Brother Amos**, the Shepherd — the true faith that exposes Vega's gilded fraud.

## Dr. Orin Pyre — "The Negation"
Domain: **Nullification.** Every hero is just a flaw he has not found yet. Dr. Orin Pyre is a Dominion villain who unmakes what others build.

## Delphine Roe — "The Oddsmaker"
Domain: **Fate.** She always knows how it ends, and she bets accordingly. Delphine Roe is a Dominion villain who trades in fate. Her rival is **Sana Okoye**, the Tidewarden — the clutch that breaks the Oddsmaker's math.
