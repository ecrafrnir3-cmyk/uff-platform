# The First War — The World

**The First War** is the founding myth of Ultimate Fantasy Football (UFF). Two ancient orders are locked in an endless war, and every season is another chapter of it.

- The **Vanguard** is the order of heroes. In the language of the real game, the Vanguard is the **AFC**. A manager who chooses the hero faction fights for the Vanguard.
- The **Dominion** is the order of villains. In the language of the real game, the Dominion is the **NFC**. A manager who chooses the villain faction fights for the Dominion.

## The premise

The war did not begin this year and it will not end this year. But this is the season a new class of legends is cast — and every manager who chooses a side is cast as one of them. When you enlist, a character is assigned to you: a name, an epithet, a domain, and a war to fight.

## The status quo at the start of Season 1

The **Dominion holds the high ground.** Roman Slate, called **The Dynasty**, sits a throne no one has taken in living memory. The **Vanguard is thinner than it has ever been** — and, its founder insists, more dangerous for it.

Every matchup is a **front** in the war. Every week decides who is winning. There is no fixed schedule of destiny here — only fronts, and the map is redrawn every Sunday night.

## The rule of the story

The ending is not written. **The season itself gets a vote.** The story of The First War is living canon, co-authored by the managers, the results on the field, and the season as it unfolds. What happens in Season 1 becomes the canon the next class inherits.

## Tone

The tone is **grounded-mythic**: these characters read like a prestige graphic novel — weathered, real, cinematic — not campy cartoons and not generic superheroes. Mythic archetypes, not athletes.

## The two layers of the story

The First War exists on two layers that mirror each other:

1. The **canon layer** — the 20 legends of the Vanguard and the Dominion, and the rivalries, betrayals, and blood-ties between them.
2. The **real-league layer** — the actual managers of the inaugural league (also named *The First War*), each cast into a canon character, carrying their own real relationships into the war.

When a real relationship echoes a canon one — brother against brother, blood divided across the line — the two layers become one story.
