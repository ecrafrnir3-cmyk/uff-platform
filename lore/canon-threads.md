# Canon Threads — The Rivalries and Blood-Ties of The First War

These are the named threads that bind the Vanguard and the Dominion together. They are the spine of the story.

## The Vale Brothers — brother against brother
**Titus Vale** (the Ironhide Sentinel, Vanguard) and **Silas Vane** (the Rust King, Dominion) are **brothers**. Titus holds the line; Silas left it to rust and defected to the Dominion. Every time the Vanguard and the Dominion meet, the Vale brothers meet across the line. This is the oldest wound in the war.

## The Betrayal — the Warden who fell
**Ezra Cain** wore Vanguard blue once. **Vesper Kane** (the Turnover Queen, Dominion) turned him — she taught him what betrayal pays — and Ezra became **The Fallen Warden**. Cain is proof that loyalty is not permanent and the line can be crossed.

## The Throne — the reign and the rebellion
**Roman Slate** (the Dynasty) holds a throne no one has taken in living memory. **Cassia Dawn** (the First Light) founded the Vanguard to break it. Their rivalry is the central axis of the war: the reign versus the rebellion.

## The Signal and the Whisper — foresight against deception
**Lyra Vann** (the Signal, Vanguard) sees the play before it is called. **Nyx Sable** (the Whisper, Dominion) makes sure you never hear her coming. Foresight against deception — they are mirror rivals.

## The recurring motif: blood divided
The war's deepest theme is **blood divided across the line** — kin who end up on opposite sides. The Vale brothers are the canon origin of this motif. In the real league, the Snapp twins become its living echo.

## The Vale Mirror — canon and real, matched role for role
The echo is exact, a one-to-one mirror: **Jason mirrors Titus Vale**, the brother who held the line (both chose the Vanguard); **Josh mirrors Silas Vane**, the brother who fell (both chose the Dominion). This correspondence is called the **Vale Mirror** — the Vale brothers of canon reborn as the real Snapp twins.

## The Unbreakable Line — Marcus Kell vs Kord Malphas
**Marcus Kell** (the Long Reckoning, Vanguard) and **Kord Malphas** (the Breaker, Dominion) are rivals — endurance against force. Kord Malphas exists to find what people lean on and break it; Marcus Kell is the load that has never broken, written off three times and still standing. Marcus Kell is the rival of Kord Malphas.

## Against the Odds — Sana Okoye vs Delphine Roe
**Sana Okoye** (the Tidewarden, Vanguard) and **Delphine Roe** (the Oddsmaker, Dominion) are rivals — clutch against fate. Delphine Roe prices every outcome and is never wrong; Sana Okoye is the clutch variable she cannot price, the calm that beats the math when the moment is biggest. Sana Okoye is the rival of Delphine Roe.

## The Shepherd and the False Saint — Brother Amos vs Saint Vega
**Brother Amos** (the Shepherd, Vanguard) and **Saint Vega** (the Gilded, Dominion) are rivals — true faith against gilded fraud. Amos leads everyone home for nothing; Vega sells the salvation Amos gives freely. Brother Amos is the rival of Saint Vega.

## Nothing Left to Take — Gideon Frost vs Countess Mave
**Gideon Frost** (the Last Stand, Vanguard) and **Countess Mave** (the Vampire, Dominion) are rivals — defiance against siphon. Countess Mave grows strong on others' strength, but defiance is not a resource to drain; Gideon Frost is most dangerous when he has nothing left — the one cup she cannot empty. Gideon Frost is the rival of Countess Mave.
