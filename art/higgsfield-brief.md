# The First War — Higgsfield Character Art Brief

Generated + adversarially refined (draft → visual-consistency critique + tone/faction critique → synthesis). Twenty portraits designed to read as **one artist's single comic issue**, differentiated by character and faction — not by style drift.

## How to use
1. Generate **1:1 (square)** portraits. Drop each into the comic page's portrait slots (the `PORTRAITS` map).
2. Paste the **full per-character prompt** below (each is standalone — it already includes the shared style + framing + faction grading).
3. Set the **negative prompt** (shared, below) on every generation.
4. Generate the **10 Vanguard** together and the **10 Dominion** together, in one sitting each, so each faction batch stays internally consistent.
5. Generate 3–4 variants per character; pick the one that best matches the framing (tight chest-up, upper-left key light, near-black void).
6. The whole set is designed so the two factions differ in **value and atmosphere** (heroes: open shadows, cool dawn light; villains: crushed shadows, crimson under-kick) — not just color. If a hero starts looking like a recolored villain, regenerate.

---

## Global Style Bible
Grounded-mythic character portraits for a prestige graphic novel, in the vein of modern Image/Vertigo comics — painterly digital illustration with confident inked linework, not glossy 3D render and not Saturday-morning cartoon. Every figure is a weathered, real human archetype rendered with tactile texture: worn leather, hammered iron, frayed cloth, scarred skin, grime, subtle pores and stubble. Dramatic single-key cinematic lighting with deep shadow modeling and controlled rim light; a muted, desaturated base palette so the two faction accent colors read as the only saturated notes in frame. Restrained, moody, mythic gravitas — quiet intensity over spectacle, no capes-and-tights superheroics, no generic Marvel/DC costuming. All twenty share one pre-industrial mythic world — iron, leather, wool, brocade, stone — with no modern signifiers. Consistent medium-heavy detail level and one unified brush language across the whole set so twenty panels read as one artist, one issue.

## Framing Spec (fixed for all 20)
1:1 square composition. Tight chest-up head-and-shoulders portrait with a sliver of upper torso, subject centered with a small margin of headroom, eyes on the upper-third line. Slight three-quarter angle (face ~15–20° off-axis, gaze toward or just past the lens), 85mm portrait lens with gentle compression and shallow depth of field. Key light from the upper left at ~45° creating a strong shadow side and a thin rim on the right edge; background is a deep, near-black atmospheric void with soft directional haze and a faint tonal gradient — no environment, no floating props. Gear is worn, never held: armor, collars, clasps, crowns, cloth at shoulder and throat are fine; NO raised hands, NO objects held up to camera, NO staff/sword/shield/cards/disc, NO prop behind or above the head. Headgear sits low on the brow, inside the headroom. No on-image text, captions, logos, watermark, or signature.

## Faction Grading
**Vanguard (heroes):** the cool cinematic dawn light IS the fixed upper-left 45° key. A relatively strong, clean key with genuine fill so shadows stay open and the mood lifts; a thin cold pale-gold rim on the right. Electric blue (#0057FF) dominant (cloak, cloth, shadow-fill); warm gold (#FFD700) secondary, restricted to metal edges/clasps/trim so it glints. Desaturated steel-grey base; faint cold dawn-blue gradient in the void. Exactly two accent hues — blue and gold, no teal/cyan/green. Weathered but resolute — grounded, never shiny or cheesy.

**Dominion (villains):** the same upper-left 45° key *direction* (so the roster aligns edge to edge) but dim and hard, almost no fill, deep crushed shadow side; a low sickly crimson under-kick from below the jaw. Thin blood-red rim on the right. Blood red (#CC0000) dominant; tarnished blackened gilt secondary, confined to corroded metal/crowns/filigree (decayed wealth). Deep matte black + oxidized brown base; denser dirtier void with a low oxblood-brown gradient and sooty haze at the bottom. Oily rust and grime in every texture. Two accent hues — blood red and tarnished gilt, no purple/magenta, orange only as literal rust. Restrained and sinister — gilded decay, never garish. **The difference from the heroes reads in value and atmosphere, not hue alone.**

## Shared Negative Prompt
> no text, no letters, no captions, no watermark, no signature, no logo, no team emblems, no sports uniforms, no jerseys, no football, no helmet facemask, no stadium; no cartoon, no anime, no cel-shading, no chibi, no cute, no campy superhero spandex, no glossy plastic 3D render, no CGI sheen; no modern signifiers, no eyeglasses, no wristwatches, no zippers, no printed cards, no clinical or surgical dress, no sportswear; no glowing eyes, no magical energy, no smoke or mist effects, no floating objects, no visible weather, no motion streaks, no held or raised props; no real celebrity likeness, no recognizable public figure; not full body, no wide shot, no multiple people, no crowd; no extra fingers, no deformed hands, no malformed face, no asymmetric eyes, no extra limbs; no oversaturated rainbow colors, no third accent hue, no teal, no cyan, no green, no purple, no magenta, no neon glow overload, no lens flare spam, no bokeh light orbs, no busy background, no clutter; no low detail, no blurry, no washed out, no flat lighting, no smiling stock-photo expression.
